# GRC Risk Frameworks & Audit Readiness

This repository contains traditional Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) artifacts demonstrating the translation of Board-level risk appetite into operational controls.

## 📊 Interactive Dashboards & Files

**[CLICK HERE to access the full GRC Portfolio (Google Drive)](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1jC129b9YVbcZr2GDXGrPf88RqHjr16Im?usp=sharing)**

### 1. Enterprise Risk Register
A Board-level risk register mapping DORA, GDPR, and EU AI Act requirements into a 5x5 scoring matrix. Features a mandatory "Impact 4" floor for regulatory risks and quantifies risk financing treatments (Reduce, Transfer, Avoid).

### 2. ISO 27001:2022 Gap Analysis
An audit readiness assessment evaluating 15 Annex A controls for a simulated Dutch financial entity. Produces a risk-rated gap register and a 30/90-day remediation roadmap aligned with NIS2 and DORA.

## Methodology
Utilised AI-assisted drafting for initial framework scaffolding, followed by human-in-the-loop validation to ensure 100% alignment with regulatory text (DORA, EU AI Act) and accurate risk scoring.
